Hyunsu Lee is an Associate Professor in the Department of Physiology and BioMedical Informatics, Pusan National University School of Medicine, and leads the NeuroAI Lab. Trained as a physician-scientist in physiology, he studies how the hippocampus learns and navigates, and applies AI to biomedical problems.
