        ## 1. AI-Accelerated Drug Discovery

        We harness deep generative models, virtual screening pipelines, and physics-based simulations to identify and validate novel therapeutics — exemplified by our FAK activator study [[1]](../publication/yoon-2025/). From hit discovery to mechanism prediction, we close the loop between in-silico hypotheses and wet-lab assays.

        ## 2. Brain Data & Neuropsychiatry

        Our interpretable machine-learning pipelines span scales: we predict neuronal genotypes purely from electrophysiology [[2]](../publication/seo-2019/), are constructing in-silico epilepsy models with NEURON and NetPyNE as part of our ongoing work, and our human cortical recordings reveal an unexpected inhibitory shift in fast-spiking interneurons in FCD I epilepsy [[3]](../publication/cho-2024/). We also analyse immune–neural crosstalk in Alzheimer's disease [[4]](../publication/kang-2025/). Insights into neurogenesis-driven pattern separation further link cellular plasticity to mood disorders [[5]](../publication/lee-2020/).

        ## 3. AI-Driven Clinical Decision Support

        Our learning engines tackle three fronts of bedside care: they predict and prevent adverse events with early bacteremia alerts and postoperative risk scores; they optimise procedures by tailoring prostate biopsy strategies and guiding lung-cancer surgery planning; and they deliver clinical language intelligence, transforming free-text notes into standardised diagnosis codes with large language models. Our highly cited perspective on ChatGPT's role in medical education (>500 citations) further demonstrates our early leadership in deploying LLMs to healthcare settings [[6]](../publication/lee-2024/).

        ## 4. Hippocampus × Reinforcement Learning

        We test the hypothesis that the hippocampus implements the Successor Representation, quantifying how neuromodulators sculpt learning rules [[7]](../publication/lee-2022/), [[8]](../publication/lee-daou-2025/), [[9]](../publication/lee-2025/). Our peer-reviewed Biosystems study [[7]](../publication/lee-2022/) provides the first empirical support for this framework. Subsequent work extends the theory: T-maze simulations reveal how predecessor and successor features transfer robustly in noisy settings [[10]](../publication/seo-2024/), and systematic analyses of weight initialisation show that carefully tuned matrix priors accelerate convergence and stability [[11]](../publication/lee-2023/).

        > "From synapse to silicon, insight travels — and patient care transforms."
