        ## 1. AI-Accelerated Drug Discovery

        We screen and design candidate compounds with virtual screening, AI-based property prediction and molecular dynamics, then validate them with wet-lab collaborators. Recent results include an FAK activator found in silico ([Comput Biol Chem 2025](../publication/yoon-2025/)) and two kinase inhibitors characterised with Keimyung University: KMU-11342 against colorectal cancer ([Pharmaceuticals 2026](../publication/jeon-2026/)) and KMU-11361 for rheumatoid arthritis ([Inflammation Research 2026](../publication/baek-2026/)). Ongoing targets include the lysosomal ion channel TMEM175 and REM sleep behaviour disorder.

        ## 2. Brain Data & Neuropsychiatry

        We build interpretable pipelines for neural data across scales. Highlights: predicting a neuron's genetic marker from its electrophysiology alone ([Brain Res Bull 2019](../publication/seo-2019/)), an inhibitory shift of fast-spiking interneurons in human FCD I epilepsy ([Nature Communications 2024](../publication/cho-2024/)), and CASCADE, an open-source pipeline that analyses multi-electrode array recordings from five manufacturers including criticality and avalanche statistics ([Bioinformatics 2026](../publication/avila-2026/)). We also study immune–neural crosstalk in Alzheimer's disease ([J Neuroimmunol 2025](../publication/kang-2025/)), EEG-based stress detection ([IEEE Access 2026](../publication/kim-2026/)), and in-silico epilepsy models in NEURON/NetPyNE.

        ## 3. AI-Driven Clinical Decision Support

        We prefer models clinicians can read. We mapped free-text EMR diagnoses to SNOMED-CT with a fine-tuned ClinicalBERT ([Med Biol Eng Comput 2026](../publication/oh-2026/)) and derived an interpretable risk equation for post-transplant bloodstream infection with symbolic regression ([Biomedicines 2026](../publication/oh-2026-2/)). Earlier work covers endoscopic image classification ([Scientific Reports 2021](../publication/lee-2021/); [J Digit Imaging 2023](../publication/shin-2023/)) and an early, widely cited perspective on ChatGPT in medical education ([Anat Sci Educ 2024](../publication/lee-2024/)).

        ## 4. Hippocampus × Reinforcement Learning

        We test whether the hippocampus implements the successor representation and how neuromodulators shape its learning rules ([Biosystems 2022](../publication/lee-2022/); [Front Comput Neurosci 2025](../publication/lee-daou-2025/)). Simulation studies examined weight initialisation ([Electronics 2023](../publication/lee-2023/)), transfer in noisy T-mazes ([Sensors 2024](../publication/seo-2024/)) and noise resilience in 1D/2D environments ([Sensors 2025](../publication/lee-2025/)). Most recently, an acetylcholine-modulated predecessor-feature model lets an agent keep exploring after reward ([Cognitive Neurodynamics 2026](../publication/seo-2026/)).
